# Starfall Arsenal

A **Minecraft mod** (Fabric, Minecraft **1.21.11**) with four cosmic weapons.
Each one has a completely different power, and each plays a **full cinematic
cutscene** first: letterbox bars, a title card, scripted camera shots, then a
white-out flash and screen shake. It's inspired by the "Shooting Star"
Minecraft mod trend.

**Web page:** https://hoai2k.github.io/francis/starfall-arsenal/ has 3D previews
of the weapons, a browser re-creation of each cutscene, and the download.

## The weapons

| Weapon | Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stellar Remote** | Orbital Lance 衛星光槍 | A reticle locks on, then a satellite fires a beam that sweeps across the ground and burns a 30-block trench |
| **Starfall Blade** | Comet Dash 彗星突 | Netherite-tier sword. You become the comet: charge through mobs, leap, and slam down with a shockwave |
| **Seven Stars Scepter** | Seven Stars 北斗七星 | Seven stars descend into the Big Dipper, lines join them, a gravity well drags mobs in, then they implode |
| **Supernova Core** | Supernova 超新星 | A star swells into a black hole that rips blocks out of the ground, collapses, and erases a 22-block sphere |

Every weapon has a hand-built 3D model (Minecraft JSON item models generated by
`mod/tools/gen_assets.py`). Blasts drop **Star Fragments**, which you craft
into the other three weapons.

## Install

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/) for Minecraft **1.21.11**.
2. Put [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) (for 1.21.11) and
   [`download/starfall-arsenal-1.1.0.jar`](download/starfall-arsenal-1.1.0.jar) in your `mods` folder.
3. The weapons are in the **Starfall Arsenal** creative tab.

## Controls

- **Right-click** with a weapon to use its skill where you're looking (the
  blade charges straight ahead). You get blast protection while the film plays.
- **J** skips the cutscene. When no film is playing, **J** turns films on or off.
- `/gamerule starfall:terrain_damage false` keeps the effects but leaves the terrain alone.

## Crafting

- **Stellar Remote**: amethyst shard (top), iron / redstone block / iron, iron / gold ingot / iron.
- **Starfall Blade**: star fragment, amethyst + star fragment + amethyst, diamond sword.
- **Seven Stars Scepter**: 7 star fragments around 2 blaze rods.
- **Supernova Core**: 8 star fragments around a nether star.

## Building the mod

```sh
cd mod
./gradlew build               # jar lands in mod/build/libs/
python3 tools/gen_assets.py   # only after changing the model/texture designs
```

The web page (`index.html`, `app.js`, `style.css`) is plain HTML/JS with
three.js from a CDN. It loads the mod's own model JSON and palette texture, so
the web previews always match the in-game models.
