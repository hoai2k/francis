# Starfall Arsenal

A **Minecraft mod** (Fabric, Minecraft 1.21.1) with four cosmic weapons that call
down shooting stars. Before every impact a **full cinematic cutscene** plays:
letterbox bars, a title card, the star igniting in the sky, a chase cam riding
the star down, then a white-out flash, camera shake, a shockwave and a smoking
crater. It's inspired by the "Shooting Star" Minecraft mod trend.

**Web page:** https://hoai2k.github.io/francis/starfall-arsenal/ has 3D previews
of the weapons, a browser re-creation of the cutscenes, and the download.

## The weapons

| Weapon | Skill | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Stellar Remote** | Shooting Star 流星 | One star, 11-block crater, 7.5 s film |
| **Starfall Blade** | Comet Slash 彗星斬 | Netherite-tier sword; right-click calls a low comet in from the horizon |
| **Seven Stars Scepter** | Seven Stars 北斗七星 | The Big Dipper falls one star at a time |
| **Supernova Core** | Supernova 超新星 | A star the size of a house, 19-block crater |

Every weapon has a hand-built 3D model (Minecraft JSON item models generated by
`mod/tools/gen_assets.py`). Every impact drops **Star Fragments**, which you
craft into the other three weapons.

## Install

1. Install [Fabric Loader](https://fabricmc.net/use/installer/) for Minecraft **1.21.1**.
2. Put [Fabric API](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api) and
   [`download/starfall-arsenal-1.0.0.jar`](download/starfall-arsenal-1.0.0.jar) in your `mods` folder.
3. The weapons are in the **Starfall Arsenal** creative tab.

## Controls

- **Right-click** with a weapon to strike where you're looking. The target is
  pushed far enough away that you aren't standing in the crater, and you get
  blast protection while the film plays.
- **J** skips the cutscene. When no film is playing, **J** turns films on or off.
  You can rebind it in Controls.
- `/gamerule starfallTerrainDamage false` keeps the blast and leaves the terrain alone.

## Crafting

- **Stellar Remote**: amethyst shard (top), iron / redstone block / iron, iron / gold ingot / iron.
- **Starfall Blade**: star fragment, amethyst + star fragment + amethyst, diamond sword.
- **Seven Stars Scepter**: 7 star fragments around 2 blaze rods.
- **Supernova Core**: 8 star fragments around a nether star.

## Building the mod

```sh
cd mod
./gradlew build          # jar lands in mod/build/libs/
python3 tools/gen_assets.py   # only after changing the model/texture designs
```

The web page (`index.html`, `app.js`, `style.css`) is plain HTML/JS with
three.js from a CDN. It loads the mod's own model JSON and palette texture, so
the web previews always match the in-game models.
