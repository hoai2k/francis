# Hakari vs Kashimo

A 2-minute Minecraft-style fan animation of Kinji Hakari vs Hajime Kashimo in
the Tokyo No. 2 Colony, following the story beats of Jujutsu Kaisen
chapters 185-190. It's all drawn with the Canvas API, and every sound is
synthesized with the Web Audio API.

Beats, in manga order: Hakari's right hook rescues Panda and sends Kashimo
through freight containers → the wager (points vs. information on Sukuna) →
Kashimo's electric cursed energy and a lightning strike that can't be dodged →
a freight container batted back and forth until it shatters → a severed arm
that regrows → **Idle Death Gamble** (Transit Card Riichi, the rules forced
into Kashimo's head, JACKPOT, the 4:11 timer) → Kashimo's knockout combo
undone by a consecutive effect → a lightning bolt to the head → the staff
through Hakari's stomach with 8 seconds left → a second domain at 1/239 odds and
a second jackpot → the barrier dragged over the sea → chlorine gas made by
electrolysis, purged by automatic reverse cursed technique → the steam explosion → Hakari's left arm
given up in a binding vow → no winner, a deal. Kashimo never uses Mythical
Beast Amber here; he's saving it for Sukuna.

Dialogue lines are short paraphrases, not quotes from the manga.

## Controls

Play/pause with the button, a tap or `Space`; drag the timeline or use `←`/`→`;
`R` restarts, `M` mutes, `F` goes fullscreen. `?t=49` opens at the jackpot.
