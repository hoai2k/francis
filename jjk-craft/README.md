# Jujutsu Craft

A 30-second, fully procedural 2D fan animation: Jujutsu Kaisen, but in blocky
Minecraft style. Everything (characters, terrain, effects, chiptune music and
sound effects) is drawn and synthesised in plain JavaScript with the Canvas and
Web Audio APIs. No images, no libraries.

Story beats: a pink-haired sorcerer meets a cursed spirit, lands a Divergent
Fist and a **Black Flash**, a special-grade curse rises, and the white-haired
strongest sorcerer shows up for **Domain Expansion: Infinite Void** and
**Hollow Purple**.

## Controls

- **Play / Pause**: the play button, tapping the picture, or `Space`
- **Scrub**: drag the timeline slider, or `←` / `→` to jump 1 second
- **Restart**: `R`
- **Sound on/off**: speaker button or `M`
- **Fullscreen**: the ⛶ button or `F`

Add `?t=12` to the URL to open paused at a given second.
