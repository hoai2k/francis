# Yuji & Todo vs Sukuna

A 2-minute Minecraft-style fan fight: Yuji Itadori and Aoi Todo against
Heian-era Sukuna (four arms, second face, mouth on his stomach) in Shinjuku. It's all drawn
with Canvas and every sound is synthesized with Web Audio.

The star is **Boogie Woogie**: every vibraslap rattle or clap swaps two things
with cursed energy. The swaps are real in the code (`resolve()` in
`js/shots.js`), so they chain: Yuji swapping with rubble he threw, Sukuna swapped
into his own Dismantle, a fake-out rattle with no swap, a 5-swap combo,
dodging Divine Flame by swapping with a rock, Black Flashes chained by swapping
Sukuna into Yuji's fist, and a final swap into the sky over the World-Cutting Slash.

Non-canon: the fight and its ending are invented. Dialogue is original.

Controls: `Space` play/pause, `←`/`→` seek, `R` restart, `M` mute, `F`
fullscreen. `?t=73` jumps to the Black Flash chain.
