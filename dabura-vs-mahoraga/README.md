# Dabura vs Mahoraga

A 2-minute Minecraft-style fan animation of **Dabura Karaba vs Divine General
Mahoraga** from *Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo*, with an alternate ending. It's drawn
with the Canvas API and every sound is synthesized with Web Audio.

The first part follows the manga's beats (Modulo ch. 15-21): Yuka Okkotsu
summons Mahoraga with Ten Shadows and hides in her shadow; Dabura's
mass-plus-murderous-intent one-shot; light beams; the wheel turning as
Mahoraga adapts; Dabura stopping the sword with one hand; the realization that
he had never had to fight; sub-light speed that tears off Mahoraga's left arm and
levels city blocks; working out reverse cursed technique; "Darkness"; his
Domain Expansion (its name isn't shown here).

**Alternate ending:** in the manga the duel is cut short when Maru teleports
Dabura away. Here nobody interrupts. Dabura folds the domain's light into a
single point of Darkness, and Mahoraga is exorcised with the wheel's last
click stuck halfway. Then Dabura uses his new reverse cursed technique to
save Yuka.

Dialogue is original, written for this animation.

## Controls

Play/pause with the button, a tap or `Space`; drag the timeline or use `←`/`→`;
`R` restarts, `M` mutes, `F` goes fullscreen. `?t=83` jumps to the finale.
