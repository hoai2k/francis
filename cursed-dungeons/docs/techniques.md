Each kit has its own melee combo, a starting technique on RMB, three more
techniques on 1 / 2 / 3 and its own upgrade nodes on the technique tree.
The select screen and the technique tree show each ability's name.

Notes on the kits:

- **Yuji's Black Flash**: press attack again right as a hit lands. The
  next blow does 2.5× damage with a black-and-red flash, black lightning,
  heavy hit-stop and a distorted shockwave.
- **The ten newest techniques** (35 in all), 20 abilities each:
  - **Dabura** (*Jujutsu Kaisen Modulo*): Light beams from his hands and feet
    that harden into constructs (Piercing Light pins curses, Light Construct
    Cage traps them), Light poured full of positive energy turning into
    Darkness (a sphere that drags curses in), killing intent made tangible
    (Intent Step, Murderous Pressure), Simurian fists, lightspeed kicks and the
    Black Rope that cancels techniques. His domain is the one he improvised
    against Mahoraga; the manga had not explained its sure-hit, so here Light
    pillars and Darkness spheres take turns striking every curse inside.
  - **Dagon**: water jets, Disaster Tides waves and a Tsunami, whirlpools,
    Death Swarm fish shikigami, piranhas, an electric eel, and sea beasts
    (shark, octopus, jellyfish, a breaching whale). The Dagon boss is unchanged.
  - **Kenjaku**: Anti-Gravity System (repulse, Weightless, Gravity Crush),
    his stocked curses (Rainbow Dragon, Smallpox Deity, Kuchisake-onna,
    Maximum: Uzumaki), curtains and barriers, and Culling Game edicts.
  - **Yorozu**: metal shards, insect armour, liquid metal and the True Sphere,
    which destroys anything it touches.
  - **Angel**: holy arrows, rays of Jacob's Ladder that cancel curses'
    attacks, wings and a halo, healing.
  - **Ishigori**: raw output blasts, Granite Blast, recoil jumps, a pompadour
    charge and a Big Bang.
  - **Uro**: she grips the sky like cloth: Sky Fold, Sky Flip, Folded
    Deflection (shots turned back), Thin Ice Breaker.
  - **Kirara**: stars of the Southern Cross stamped on curses; Rendezvous
    crashes them together in the constellation's order, Repulsion keeps
    stamped curses away.
  - **Ui Ui**: teleports curses (Sky Drop, Scramble, Gathering, Telefrag),
    himself and his allies, with Mei Mei's crows on call.
  - **Takaba**: washtubs, banana peels, pies and squeaky mallets, a manzai
    partner, and Not Funny (nothing hurts him while he isn't laughing).
- **Domain Expansion** (and every ultimate) needs a full Cursed Energy bar. After a short hand
  sign (no camera move), a **barrier** closes around the caster and everyone
  nearby. Nobody can cross it until it falls: curses, bosses, allies and
  you stay inside, and projectiles stop at the wall. Inside, the world
  becomes the domain (floor, sky, lighting, set-pieces); from outside it is
  a black sphere. A domain lasts 10 to 20 seconds (a timer bar sits under
  its name), then the barrier cracks and shatters. Malevolent Shrine is an
  open domain: no barrier, but a huge reach. Boss domains trap you in the
  same way until they end or the boss dies. Only one domain can stand at a
  time; in a clash the more refined domain wins.
- Each sorcerer has a **skill tree**. Points come from levelling up.
